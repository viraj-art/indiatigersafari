Luxury Tiger Safari in India

A luxury tiger safari in India is a quiet, beautifully paced journey: dawn drives with a naturalist who knows the forest by heart, unhurried middays at an exceptional lodge, and evenings by the fire. It is as much about the craft of tracking as the sighting itself, and every detail, from the transfers to the table, is arranged so that you may simply be present in the wild.

A Day on Safari

Your morning begins before sunrise with tea or coffee, then a three- to four-hour drive in an open vehicle as the forest wakes. Guides read alarm calls, pugmarks and the movements of deer to anticipate where a tiger may appear. Returning to the lodge for a leisurely breakfast and a restful midday, you set out again in the golden light of late afternoon. Most guests enjoy two drives a day, usually six to eight across a stay.

Where You Will Stay

India's finest safari lodges sit on the edge of the forest, designed to feel part of it. Think private villas and tented suites, plunge pools, spa treatments, and cuisine prepared with local ingredients. We work with trusted names such as Taj Safaris, Pugdundee Safaris, Aman, Oberoi, Sujan and The Postcard, properties we have used for many years, and our safari specialists can find the ideal stay for your preferences.

Your Naturalist and Your Vehicle

Resident naturalists bring a lifetime of local knowledge, from the personalities of individual tigers to the calls of the smaller birds. Most parks require safaris in open vehicles within allocated zones, and we arrange private vehicles and guides wherever possible so the pace stays yours. Overland journeys between parks are made in comfortable, chauffeur-driven private SUVs, and domestic flights are included.

Beyond the Tiger

The forest offers far more than its most famous resident. Leopard, sloth bear, dhole, gaur, barasingha and over 250 species of birds may share a single morning. Between drives, lodges can arrange nature walks, village visits, cultural encounters, wildlife photography masterclasses and, in the right season, private dinners in the forest.

Travelling Responsibly

Tiger tourism supports the communities and rangers who protect these landscapes. Staying in small, thoughtfully run lodges, travelling in limited numbers and following the guidance of park rules all help the forest thrive. Every journey we arrange contributes to TOFTigers, winner of a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025.

Bringing It Together

Many guests pair their safari with the Golden Triangle of Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, staying in the finest hotels in each city, with a VIP meet and greet on arrival. In every city our private concierge secures reservations at the finest restaurants, including hard-to-book tables, and can arrange private dinners for special occasions. To begin, our safari specialists will curate a bespoke itinerary around your dates, interests and preferred pace.

Luxury Tiger Safari in India FAQs

What does a luxury tiger safari in India include?

Our journeys include handpicked lodges on a full-board basis, expert naturalists, private transfers, domestic flights and your safari drives.

How many tigers will I see?

Tigers are wild and elusive, so sightings vary. Spending four nights or more in each park gives you the best chance, and each drive is rewarding in its own right.

What should I wear on safari?

Choose muted greens, khaki and brown in layers, as mornings can be cool and middays warm. A hat, sunglasses and binoculars are welcome.

Is it suitable for children and multi-generational families?

Yes. We adapt the pace and activities, and arrange private vehicles so families can enjoy the forest together.

How do I book?

Speak with our safari specialists, who will design a private, bespoke itinerary and take care of every detail from arrival to departure.
