About This Golden Triangle Tour

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur form India's Golden Triangle, three cities that together tell the story of the Mughal emperors and the Rajput kings. In a few unhurried days, this Golden Triangle tour takes you from the grand avenues and ancient bazaars of Delhi to the Taj Mahal at sunrise, then on to the rose-pink city of Jaipur and the hilltop ramparts of Amber Fort. It is the perfect complement to a tiger safari, adding a chapter of royal history to your time in the wild.

Each city is revealed by a private historian, with early starts timed to beat the crowds and afternoons left free to rest or explore. You stay at three of India's finest hotels: The Oberoi New Delhi; The Oberoi Amarvilas, where every room looks out to the Taj Mahal; and Rambagh Palace, once the home of the Maharaja of Jaipur. A VIP welcome at Delhi airport and chauffeur-driven private SUVs make every transfer effortless.

We travel responsibly. In Jaipur you ascend Amber Fort by private vehicle rather than by elephant, in keeping with our commitment to ethical wildlife travel. Your journey also contributes to TOFTigers, the wildlife tourism and conservation alliance that won a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025. The best time to travel is October to March, when days are warm and evenings cool, and every departure is private and bespoke, curated around your dates and interests.

Golden Triangle Tour Itinerary: Day by Day

  1. Day 1: New Delhi

    A VIP representative meets you on arrival and guides you swiftly through the airport or, if you are joining from one of our safaris, your chauffeur collects you from your hotel. Check in to The Oberoi New Delhi, whose rooms look out over the greens of the Delhi Golf Club. In the late afternoon, a private historian introduces you to the city at Humayun's Tomb, the garden tomb that inspired the Taj Mahal, before a drive past India Gate and the grand avenues of Lutyens' Delhi.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  2. Day 2: New Delhi to Agra

    Begin in Old Delhi, where a cycle-rickshaw carries you through the spice market and lanes of Chandni Chowk to the Jama Masjid, one of India's largest mosques. After lunch, drive to Agra on the Yamuna Expressway, about three and a half hours. At The Oberoi Amarvilas, just 600 metres from the Taj Mahal, every room looks out to the monument, and the evening is best spent on your balcony as it glows in the setting sun.

    Overnight: The Oberoi Amarvilas, Agra

  3. Day 3: Agra to Jaipur

    Rise before dawn for the short ride by private buggy to the Taj Mahal, timed to watch the marble turn from grey to gold as the sun comes up. Return for breakfast, then explore Agra Fort, the red-sandstone citadel of the Mughal emperors. Later, drive to Jaipur, about five hours, pausing at Fatehpur Sikri, the abandoned capital of the emperor Akbar. You arrive at Rambagh Palace, once the home of the Maharaja of Jaipur, in time for dinner.

    Overnight: Rambagh Palace, Jaipur

  4. Day 4: Jaipur

    A private historian guides you through the city's great monuments. The morning begins at Amber Fort, which you ascend by private vehicle, and continues through the courtyards of the City Palace and the giant astronomical instruments of Jantar Mantar, with a pause before the honeycomb façade of the Hawa Mahal. The afternoon is yours to shape from the leisure menu, and the evening to enjoy the palace gardens as peacocks wander across the lawns.

    Overnight: Rambagh Palace, Jaipur

  5. Day 5: Jaipur to New Delhi

    After breakfast, your chauffeur drives you back to Delhi, about five hours, or to Jaipur airport for a short flight onward. You might instead continue to Ranthambore for tigers, just three and a half hours away.

A note from our planners

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays, so we arrange your days around it. October to March brings the most comfortable weather; from April to June the days are hot, and early starts make the most of the cooler hours.

Every property on this journey has been handpicked by our team. They are lodges and hotels we have worked with and returned to over many years, chosen for their setting, the quality of their naturalists, the warmth of their service and their care for the forests and communities around them. We recommend only places we know intimately, so you can rest assured that each stay will be as exceptional as the wilderness beyond its doors.   • Agra – The Oberoi Amarvilas (Day 2): Just 600 metres from the Taj Mahal, with views of the monument from every room, set among terraced lawns, reflection pools and pavilions inspired by Mughal palaces.

  • The Imperial: A grand 1930s landmark on Janpath, blending colonial and Art Deco style, with one of the city's finest collections of colonial-era art.
  • The Lodhi: A serene urban retreat near Lodhi Gardens, with generous suites and a tranquil, resort-like atmosphere.
  • Roseate House: A contemporary hotel in Aerocity, minutes from the airport.

Agra

  • ITC Mughal: A Luxury Collection hotel set in extensive Mughal-style gardens, home to the celebrated Kaya Kalp spa.

Jaipur

  • Jai Mahal Palace: An 18th-century palace surrounded by formal Mughal gardens, offering heritage charm close to the old city.
  • ITC Rajputana: Contemporary luxury inspired by Rajput architecture, conveniently placed in the heart of Jaipur.

UNESCO World Heritage Sites

  • Humayun's Tomb, Delhi: The 16th-century garden tomb that set the pattern for the Taj Mahal.
  • Taj Mahal, Agra: Shah Jahan's white marble memorial to his wife Mumtaz Mahal, and the world's most celebrated monument to love.
  • Agra Fort: The red-sandstone citadel from which the Mughal emperors ruled.
  • Fatehpur Sikri: The capital Akbar built and abandoned within a generation, preserved almost intact.
  • Amber Fort, Jaipur: One of the Hill Forts of Rajasthan, rising above Maota Lake.
  • Jantar Mantar, Jaipur: An 18th-century observatory of giant stone astronomical instruments.
  • The Walled City of Jaipur: The planned, rose-pink city of Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II.

Golden Triangle Tour FAQs

What is India's Golden Triangle?

The Golden Triangle links Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, three of India's great historic cities. Together they hold some of the country's most celebrated monuments, including the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Amber Fort.

Is the Taj Mahal open every day?

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays, so we plan your sunrise visit around it.

When is the best time for a Golden Triangle tour?

October to March, when days are warm and evenings cool. From April to June the days are hot, and early starts make the most of the cooler hours.

How much driving is involved?

Delhi to Agra takes about three and a half hours on the Yamuna Expressway, and Agra to Jaipur about five hours, including a stop at Fatehpur Sikri. From Jaipur you can drive back to Delhi in about five hours or take a one-hour flight.

Can I add a tiger safari?

Yes. Ranthambore is about three and a half hours from Jaipur, making this Golden Triangle tour a natural partner for any of our tiger safaris.
