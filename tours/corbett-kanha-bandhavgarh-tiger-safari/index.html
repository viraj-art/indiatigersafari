About This Tiger Safari

This Corbett, Kanha and Bandhavgarh tiger safari brings three of India's great tiger forests together in one unhurried journey. You begin in the Himalayan foothills at Jim Corbett, India's first national park, where sal forest gives way to river grassland and wild elephants move along the banks of the Ramganga. Next come the sweeping meadows of Kanha, where the rare hard-ground barasingha grazes in the morning mist. The journey ends beneath the sandstone cliffs of Bandhavgarh, a fort-crowned reserve with one of the highest densities of tigers in India.

Your days follow the rhythm of the forest. You set out at dawn and again in the late afternoon in a private 4x4 jeep, reserved for your party alone, with a naturalist who reads alarm calls and pugmarks and knows the resident tigers individually. The hours in between are spent at handpicked lodges, from the thatched Jungle Cottages of Jim's Jungle Retreat to the Taj Safaris lodges of Kanha and Bandhavgarh. A VIP welcome at Delhi airport, chauffeur-driven private SUVs and domestic flights make every transfer effortless.

This is travel that gives back. Our partner lodges employ and train people from neighbouring villages, our naturalists follow the forest department's code of conduct at every sighting, and your park fees help protect the reserves themselves. Your journey also contributes to TOFTigers, the wildlife tourism and conservation alliance that won a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025. The best time to travel is November to June, and every departure is private and bespoke, curated around your dates and interests.

What You Will Experience

  • Three celebrated tiger reserves in one journey: the Himalayan foothills of Corbett, the grassland meadows of Kanha and the ancient hills of Bandhavgarh.
  • Private 4x4 jeep safaris at dawn and dusk, reserved for your party alone and led by expert naturalists.

Corbett, Kanha & Bandhavgarh Itinerary: Day by Day

  1. Day 1: Arrival in New Delhi

    Your journey begins the moment you land. A VIP representative meets you as you leave the aircraft and guides you swiftly through immigration, baggage reclaim and customs. Your chauffeur then drives you in a comfortable private SUV to The Oberoi New Delhi, about 45 minutes away, where the rooms look out over the greens of the Delhi Golf Club. The rest of the day is yours to rest after your flight, or to dine at one of the hotel's acclaimed restaurants.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  2. Day 2: New Delhi to Jim Corbett National Park

    After breakfast, your chauffeur heads north-east across the plains and into the forested foothills of Kumaon, a drive of about six hours. By mid-afternoon you arrive at Jim's Jungle Retreat, on the edge of the reserve, and settle into your private Jungle Cottage, with its thatched roof, locally crafted furniture and colonial-era lithographs. Over tea, your naturalist introduces the park, its zones and the etiquette of the forest. As the light softens, a guided walk along the forest edge brings the first calls of the evening.

    Overnight: Jim's Jungle Retreat (Jungle Cottage), Jim Corbett

  3. Day 3: Jim Corbett National Park

    Rise before dawn for a private jeep safari into one of Corbett's zones, such as Dhela, Jhirna or Bijrani. As the mist lifts off the sal forest, your naturalist listens for the alarm call of a sambar or langur, often the first sign of a tiger nearby. Return for a late breakfast and an unhurried afternoon by the pool or in the spa. A second safari follows in the late afternoon, when the forest glows in the golden hour.

    Overnight: Jim's Jungle Retreat (Jungle Cottage), Jim Corbett

  4. Day 4: Jim Corbett National Park

    A dawn safari explores a different zone, with the chance of elephant herds, spotted deer and some of the hundreds of bird species that make Corbett a birder's paradise. The afternoon is yours to shape from the leisure menu. You might visit Jim Corbett's former winter home at Kaladhungi, now a museum to the hunter who became one of India's first conservationists, or the Garjia Devi temple, perched on a rock in the Kosi River. An extra afternoon safari can also be arranged.

    Overnight: Jim's Jungle Retreat (Jungle Cottage), Jim Corbett

  5. Day 5: Jim Corbett to New Delhi

    Take a final dawn safari if you wish, then return to the capital after breakfast, a drive of about six hours. Tonight you stay at Roseate House in Aerocity, minutes from the airport, so tomorrow's morning flight begins without a moment's rush.

    Overnight: Roseate House, New Delhi

  6. Day 6: New Delhi to Kanha National Park

    A short morning flight takes you to Jabalpur, where your chauffeur is waiting for the four-to-five-hour drive into the Maikal hills. You reach Banjaar Tola in the afternoon. Its tented suites line the banks of the Banjar River and look across to the forests of Kanha. Settle onto your private deck with tea, then end the day with dinner beneath the stars.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  7. Day 7: Kanha National Park

    Kanha's core has four zones, Kisli, Kanha, Mukki and Sarhi, each with its own character. This morning's safari crosses mist-covered meadows where barasingha and chital graze and packs of dhole hunt through the long grass. The heat of the day is for lunch and rest at the lodge. In the afternoon, your drive can end at Bamni Dadar, a plateau with sweeping views across the forest at sunset.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  8. Day 8: Kanha National Park

    Morning and afternoon safaris explore a different part of the park. For dedicated wildlife lovers, a full-day permit allows uninterrupted time in the forest from dawn to dusk, subject to availability. In the evening, dinner is served by lantern light in the lodge grounds.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  9. Day 9: Kanha National Park

    Enjoy a final morning safari in Kanha. In the afternoon, walk to a neighbouring Baiga or Gond village with a local guide, paint alongside a Gond artist, or simply relax on your deck above the river.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  10. Day 10: Kanha to Bandhavgarh National Park

    After an optional early safari and breakfast, a five-to-six-hour drive through the farmland of rural Madhya Pradesh brings you to Mahua Kothi. This Taj Safaris lodge has private cottages modelled on Central Indian village homes, set among mahua trees. You arrive in time for sundowners as the light turns amber on the hills.

    Overnight: Mahua Kothi (Taj Safaris), Bandhavgarh

  11. Day 11: Bandhavgarh National Park

    Morning and afternoon safaris explore zones such as Tala, Magadhi and Khitauli. In Tala, the tracks wind beneath the cliffs of the ancient fort and past Shesh Shaiya, a reclining stone Vishnu at the source of the Charanganga stream. Your naturalist shares the stories of the tigers who roam here today, many descended from legends such as Charger and Sita.

    Overnight: Mahua Kothi (Taj Safaris), Bandhavgarh

  12. Day 12: Bandhavgarh National Park

    A final full day of safaris gives you the best chance of the sightings Bandhavgarh is famous for, from tigresses with cubs to leopards and sloth bears. In the evening, a private farewell dinner is served by candlelight, with your naturalist sharing stories from a lifetime in the forest.

    Overnight: Mahua Kothi (Taj Safaris), Bandhavgarh

  13. Day 13: Bandhavgarh to New Delhi

    After a leisurely breakfast, your chauffeur drives you to Jabalpur, about four hours away, for your flight to Delhi. If flight times allow, pause at Bhedaghat, where the Narmada River flows between gleaming marble cliffs. You spend your final evening at The Oberoi New Delhi.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  14. Day 14: Departure

    Your chauffeur drives you to Indira Gandhi International Airport for your onward journey.

A note from our planners

National Parks & Wildlife Reserves

  • Jim Corbett National Park: Founded in 1936 as India's first national park, Corbett is also where Project Tiger was launched in 1973. It lies in the Himalayan foothills, where sal forest, riverine woodland and open grasslands follow the Ramganga and Kosi rivers. The park is home to tigers, wild elephant herds and river-dwelling gharial, as well as some of the richest birdlife in India.
  • Kanha National Park: Kanha spreads across the Maikal hills of the Satpura range, with tall sal forest, bamboo thickets and vast open meadows. It is often linked with the landscapes of Kipling's The Jungle Book. The park is celebrated for bringing the hard-ground barasingha back from the brink, and it offers superb sightings of tigers, dhole and gaur.
  • Bandhavgarh National Park: Bandhavgarh is set among the rugged hills of the Vindhya range, where sandstone cliffs rise above sal forest and grassy valleys. Its ancient fort, rock-cut caves and legends from the Ramayana give it a rich sense of history. It is also known for one of the highest tiger densities in India.

Tiger Safari FAQs

When is the best time for a tiger safari in Corbett, Kanha and Bandhavgarh?

November to June. The parks reopen after the monsoon, and while winter brings cool, comfortable days, the warmer months from April to June often bring the best tiger sightings as animals gather near water.

What are my chances of seeing a tiger?

Tigers are wild, so sightings can never be guaranteed. Bandhavgarh has one of the highest tiger densities in India, however, and several days in each of three reserves with expert naturalists give you excellent chances.

Are the parks open every day?

Kanha and Bandhavgarh close to safaris on Wednesday afternoons and for the festivals of Holi and Diwali. We plan your safaris around these dates.

Is this tiger safari suitable for families?

Yes. We tailor the pace, lodges and activities for families, and our naturalists love sharing the forest with young explorers.

How far in advance should I book?

Safari permits are limited and sell out quickly for peak dates, so we recommend booking four to six months ahead.
