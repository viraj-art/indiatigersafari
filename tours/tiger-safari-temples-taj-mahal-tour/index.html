About This Tiger Safari & Temples Tour

This tiger safari and Taj Mahal tour brings together three of India's greatest treasures in one unhurried journey: the Mughal splendour of Agra, the sculpted temples of Khajuraho and the tiger forests of Central India. You begin at the Taj Mahal, seen at sunrise and again from across the Yamuna at dusk, before travelling by train into Bundelkhand to the forgotten royal city of Orchha. At Khajuraho, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, you explore thousand-year-old temples carved with extraordinary grace, then head south to Bandhavgarh and Kanha, two of the finest tiger reserves in the world.

Each stay has been chosen for its sense of place. You look out over the Taj from every room at The Oberoi Amarvilas, stay steps from the Western Group of temples at The Lalit Temple View, and spend your safari nights at The Oberoi Vindhyavilas in Bandhavgarh and Banjaar Tola, a Taj Safaris camp on the river in Kanha. Every safari is taken in a private 4x4 jeep, reserved for your party alone, with naturalists who know each forest intimately, while expert guides bring the monuments to life. A VIP welcome at Delhi airport, chauffeur-driven private SUVs, an executive-class train journey and domestic flights make every transfer effortless.

This is travel that gives back. Our partner lodges employ and train people from neighbouring villages, our naturalists follow the forest department's code of conduct at every sighting, and your park fees help protect the reserves themselves. Your journey also contributes to TOFTigers, the wildlife tourism and conservation alliance that won a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025. The best time to travel is October to March, and every departure is private and bespoke, curated around your dates and interests.

What You Will Experience

  • The Taj Mahal at sunrise, with every room at The Oberoi Amarvilas looking out over its marble dome.
  • Orchha, a 16th-century Bundela capital of palaces, temples and riverside cenotaphs on the Betwa River.
  • The temples of Khajuraho, a UNESCO World Heritage Site celebrated for some of the finest stone carving in India.
  • Private 4x4 jeep safaris at dawn and dusk in Bandhavgarh and Kanha, reserved for your party alone.
  • Bandhavgarh's ancient hills, crowned by a 2,000-year-old fort and famed for one of India's highest tiger densities.
  • Kanha's great meadows, where the rare hard-ground barasingha grazes in the morning mist.
  • Expert guides at every monument and naturalists who know each forest and its tigers intimately.

Taj Mahal, Khajuraho & Tiger Safari Itinerary: Day by Day

  1. Day 1: Arrival in New Delhi

    A VIP representative meets you at the aircraft and guides you through immigration, baggage reclaim and customs. Your chauffeur drives you to The Oberoi New Delhi, where the rest of the day is yours to settle in after your flight. Dine at the hotel, or let our concierge arrange a table in the city.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  2. Day 2: New Delhi to Agra

    After breakfast, drive south to Agra along the Yamuna Expressway. In the afternoon, your guide leads you through the red sandstone walls of Agra Fort, seat of the Mughal emperors, where Shah Jahan spent his final years gazing towards the tomb he built for his wife. At sunset, cross the river to the Mehtab Bagh gardens for your first view of the Taj Mahal as its marble turns gold and rose.

    Overnight: The Oberoi Amarvilas, Agra

  3. Day 3: Agra: Taj Mahal and Fatehpur Sikri

    Rise before dawn for a private guided visit to the Taj Mahal as the first light falls on the dome, before the crowds arrive. Return for breakfast on your terrace, then drive about an hour to Fatehpur Sikri, the abandoned red sandstone capital built by Emperor Akbar in the 16th century. Spend the late afternoon by the pool, with the Taj in view.

    Overnight: The Oberoi Amarvilas, Agra

  4. Day 4: Agra to Orchha

    A morning express train carries you south to Jhansi in about two and a half hours, and your chauffeur drives you the short distance on to Orchha. Founded by the Bundela kings in the 16th century on an island in the Betwa River, Orchha is a city of palaces and temples left almost untouched by time. Explore the Raja Mahal and the Jahangir Mahal, built for the Mughal emperor's visit, then join the evening aarti at the Ram Raja Temple, the only temple in India where Rama is worshipped as a king.

    Overnight: Amar Mahal, Orchha

  5. Day 5: Orchha to Khajuraho

    Walk at dawn to the royal cenotaphs on the banks of the Betwa, where vultures nest on the domes above the river. After breakfast, drive east for three and a half to four hours to Khajuraho. In the afternoon, visit the Eastern Group of temples, including the elegant Jain temples of Parsvanath and Adinath.

    Overnight: The Lalit Temple View, Khajuraho

  6. Day 6: Khajuraho Temples

    The Chandela kings built some 85 temples here between the 10th and 12th centuries, and around 25 survive. With your guide, explore the Western Group in the soft morning light, from the soaring Kandariya Mahadeva to the Lakshmana temple, their walls alive with gods, dancers, musicians and lovers carved in sandstone. In the afternoon, drive to the Ken Gharial Sanctuary and the Raneh Falls, where the river cuts a canyon of pink and grey granite.

    Overnight: The Lalit Temple View, Khajuraho

  7. Day 7: Khajuraho to Bandhavgarh National Park

    After breakfast, drive south for five to six hours through the farmland and forests of Madhya Pradesh to Bandhavgarh. The Oberoi Vindhyavilas lies in the Vindhya hills, its luxury tents and villas set among the trees, about 10 km from the park's Tala gate. Settle in with tea on your deck as the light falls over the forest.

    Overnight: The Oberoi Vindhyavilas, Bandhavgarh

  8. Day 8: Bandhavgarh National Park

    Morning and afternoon safaris explore Bandhavgarh's sal forest, bamboo and grassy valleys. In the Tala zone, the track climbs beneath the sandstone cliffs of the ancient fort to Shesh Shaiya, a reclining stone Vishnu watched over by langurs. Your naturalist reads every alarm call for signs of tiger, with leopards, sloth bears and dhole also in these forests.

    Overnight: The Oberoi Vindhyavilas, Bandhavgarh

  9. Day 9: Bandhavgarh National Park

    A second full day of safaris, perhaps in the Magadhi or Khitauli zones, gives you an excellent chance of the tigers Bandhavgarh is celebrated for. Return in the afternoon for a treatment at the spa before a private dinner beneath the stars.

    Overnight: The Oberoi Vindhyavilas, Bandhavgarh

  10. Day 10: Bandhavgarh to Kanha National Park

    After an optional early safari, drive south for five to six hours to Kanha. Banjaar Tola sits on the banks of the Banjar River, its tented suites looking across the water to the park. Arrive in time for sundowners on your deck.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  11. Day 11: Kanha National Park

    Kanha's meadows and towering sal forest are among the most beautiful landscapes in India. Morning and afternoon safaris reveal herds of hard-ground barasingha, a deer this park saved from extinction, alongside chital, gaur and dhole, and with luck a tiger crossing the open grassland.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  12. Day 12: Kanha National Park

    A final day of safaris, perhaps ending at Bamni Dadar, a high plateau with sweeping views at sunset. In the afternoon, you might instead visit a Baiga or Gond village with a local guide. Your last evening ends with a farewell dinner by lantern light on the riverbank.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  13. Day 13: Kanha to New Delhi

    After breakfast, your chauffeur drives you to Jabalpur, about four to five hours away, for your flight to Delhi. Your final evening is at The Oberoi New Delhi.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  14. Day 14: Departure

    Your chauffeur drives you to Indira Gandhi International Airport for your onward journey.

A note from our planners

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays, and the Madhya Pradesh tiger reserves rest on Wednesday afternoons and close for Holi and Diwali. Your itinerary is arranged around these dates, with an in-lodge experience in place of any affected safari.

Your Accommodation

Every property on this journey has been handpicked by our team. They are lodges and hotels we have worked with and returned to over many years, chosen for their setting, the quality of their naturalists, the warmth of their service and their care for the forests and communities around them. We recommend only places we know intimately, so you can rest assured that each stay will be as exceptional as the wilderness beyond its doors. If you have something particular in mind, our safari specialists will gladly find the perfect stay for your needs, including properties beyond those listed here.

  • New Delhi – The Oberoi New Delhi (Days 1 & 13): A landmark of Indian hospitality, with polished service and views across the Delhi Golf Club.

  • Agra – The Oberoi Amarvilas (Days 2–3): Moorish and Mughal architecture, terraced fountains and an uninterrupted view of the Taj Mahal from every room.

  • Orchha – Amar Mahal (Day 4): A palace-style hotel built in the Bundela tradition, with domed rooms set around courtyards and gardens.

  • Khajuraho – The Lalit Temple View (Days 5–6): A contemporary luxury hotel facing the Western Group of temples, a short walk from the monuments.

  • Bandhavgarh – The Oberoi Vindhyavilas (Days 7–9): Luxury tents and two villas with private pools in the Vindhya hills, about 10 km from the Tala gate.

  • Kanha – Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris) (Days 10–12): Eighteen tented suites in two intimate camps on the banks of the Banjar River, facing the park.

National Parks & Wildlife Reserves

  • Bandhavgarh National Park: A compact reserve of rugged hills, sal forest and grassy valleys, overlooked by an ancient fort linked in legend to the Ramayana. It has one of the highest tiger densities in India.
  • Kanha National Park: One of Asia's great protected landscapes, pairing towering sal forest with sweeping meadows in the Maikal hills. It is celebrated for saving the hard-ground barasingha from extinction.
  • Ken Gharial Sanctuary: A protected stretch of the Ken River near Khajuraho, where the river carves a dramatic granite canyon at the Raneh Falls.

UNESCO World Heritage Sites

  • Taj Mahal: Built by Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, the white marble mausoleum is the jewel of Mughal architecture.
  • Agra Fort: The great red sandstone citadel of the Mughal emperors, with palaces and audience halls behind its walls.
  • Fatehpur Sikri: Akbar's short-lived capital, a remarkably preserved city of palaces, courtyards and one of India's grandest mosques.
  • Khajuraho Group of Monuments: Hindu and Jain temples built by the Chandela kings, famed for the beauty and detail of their sculpture.

Tiger Safari, Temples & Taj Mahal Tour FAQs

When is the best time for a tiger safari and Taj Mahal tour?

October to March is ideal, with cool, clear days for sightseeing and comfortable safari mornings. The tiger reserves stay open until June, and the hotter months from April are often the most rewarding for sightings, as animals gather near water.

What are my chances of seeing a tiger?

Tigers are wild, so a sighting can never be guaranteed. With three nights each in Bandhavgarh and Kanha, two of India's finest tiger reserves, and expert naturalists at your side, your chances are excellent.

How do we travel between Agra, Orchha and Khajuraho?

You take an express train from Agra to Jhansi, then travel on by private car to Orchha and Khajuraho. Your chauffeur continues with you to Bandhavgarh and Kanha, and a short flight from Jabalpur returns you to Delhi.

Is this tour suitable for first-time visitors to India?

Yes. It combines India's most famous monument with two of its finest tiger reserves at an unhurried pace, with expert guides throughout. Our safari specialists can adjust the pace or add rest days for families and younger travellers.

How do I book a private tiger safari and Taj Mahal tour?

Speak with our safari specialists, who will tailor this itinerary around your dates, interests and pace. Every journey is private, bespoke and curated for you, and we recommend booking six to nine months ahead for the best choice of hotels, lodges and park permits.
