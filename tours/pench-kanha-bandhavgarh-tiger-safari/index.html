About This Tiger Safari

This Pench, Kanha and Bandhavgarh tiger safari takes you into the heart of Central India, where three of the country's most celebrated reserves lie within a day's drive of one another. Each has a character of its own: the teak forests and winding river of Pench, the vast meadows of Kanha and the sandstone hills of Bandhavgarh. Together they offer some of the finest tiger viewing in the world.

Rather than hurrying between parks, you spend four nights each in Kanha and Bandhavgarh, with time for repeated morning and afternoon safaris and a far deeper understanding of the forest. Every drive is taken in a private 4x4 jeep, reserved for your party alone, with naturalists who know each reserve and its resident tigers intimately. You stay at three of Central India's finest safari lodges: Pench Tree Lodge in Pench, Banjaar Tola in Kanha and Mahua Kothi in Bandhavgarh. A VIP welcome at Delhi airport, chauffeur-driven private SUVs and domestic flights make every transfer effortless.

This is travel that gives back. Our partner lodges employ and train people from neighbouring villages, our naturalists follow the forest department's code of conduct at every sighting, and your park fees help protect the reserves themselves. Your journey also contributes to TOFTigers, the wildlife tourism and conservation alliance that won a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025. The parks are open from October to June, and every departure is private and bespoke, curated around your dates and interests.

What You Will Experience

  • Three of Central India's finest tiger reserves, Pench, Kanha and Bandhavgarh, linked in one seamless route with no backtracking.
  • Four nights each in Kanha and Bandhavgarh, for unhurried safaris and the best chance of exceptional sightings.
  • Private 4x4 jeep safaris at dawn and dusk, reserved for your party alone and led by expert naturalists.
  • The teak forests of Pench, the country Kipling chose for The Jungle Book, home to leopards and packs of dhole.
  • Kanha's great meadows, where the rare hard-ground barasingha grazes beside herds of chital and gaur.
  • Bandhavgarh's ancient hills, crowned by a 2,000-year-old fort and famed for one of India's highest tiger densities.
  • Three celebrated safari lodges: the tree houses of Pench Tree Lodge, the riverside tented suites of Banjaar Tola and the village-style cottages of Mahua Kothi.

Pench, Kanha & Bandhavgarh Itinerary: Day by Day

  1. Day 1: Arrival in New Delhi

    A VIP representative meets you as you leave the aircraft and guides you swiftly through immigration, baggage reclaim and customs. Your chauffeur then drives you to The Oberoi New Delhi, about 45 minutes away. Spend the afternoon at rest, perhaps with a treatment at the spa, and enjoy a quiet dinner at the hotel ahead of tomorrow's early flight.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  2. Day 2: New Delhi to Pench National Park

    An early flight takes you south to Nagpur, in the very centre of India, where your chauffeur is waiting for the drive of three to four hours to Pench. Pench Tree Lodge stands on 40 acres of private forest near the quiet Karmajhiri gate, its tree houses raised among the branches. In the afternoon, set out on your first safari through the teak forests Kipling chose as the setting for The Jungle Book.

    Overnight: Pench Tree Lodge (Pugdundee Safaris), Pench

  3. Day 3: Pench National Park

    A full day of morning and afternoon safaris along the Pench River, which winds through the heart of the park. Midway through the morning safari, stop at a designated spot in the forest for a breakfast hamper from the lodge kitchen. Spotted deer and sambar gather on the open riverbanks, drawing leopards, dhole and tigers, and your naturalist shares the story of Collarwali, the Pench tigress celebrated for raising 29 cubs. Back at the lodge, dinner is served by the fire, with produce from its organic garden.

    Overnight: Pench Tree Lodge (Pugdundee Safaris), Pench

  4. Day 4: Pench to Kanha National Park

    After an optional dawn safari and breakfast, drive east for four to five hours through the farmland and forests of Madhya Pradesh. Banjaar Tola sits on the banks of the Banjar River, its 18 tented suites set in two intimate camps that look across the water to the park. Settle onto your deck with tea as the evening light falls across the sal trees.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  5. Day 5: Kanha National Park

    Kanha's core is divided into four zones, Kisli, Kanha, Mukki and Sarhi, and your safaris move between them to reveal the park's full range. The morning drive crosses dew-covered meadows where hard-ground barasingha graze, a deer Kanha saved from the brink of extinction. In the afternoon, watch for gaur at the forest edge and dhole hunting through the long grass.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  6. Day 6: Kanha National Park

    Another day of morning and afternoon safaris, perhaps ending at Bamni Dadar, a high plateau where sambar and gaur browse against sweeping views at sunset. For dedicated wildlife lovers, a full-day permit allows uninterrupted time in the forest from dawn to dusk, subject to availability.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  7. Day 7: Kanha National Park

    Enjoy a final morning safari in Kanha. In the afternoon, walk to a neighbouring Baiga or Gond village with a local guide, or learn the intricate dot-and-line style of Gond painting from a village artist. Dinner is served by lantern light on the riverbank.

    Overnight: Banjaar Tola (Taj Safaris), Kanha

  8. Day 8: Kanha to Bandhavgarh National Park

    After an optional early safari, drive north for five to six hours to Bandhavgarh. Mahua Kothi, a Taj Safaris lodge, welcomes you with private cottages modelled on the village homes of Central India and set among mahua trees. You arrive in time for sundowners as the light turns amber on the hills.

    Overnight: Mahua Kothi (Taj Safaris), Bandhavgarh

  9. Day 9: Bandhavgarh National Park

    Morning and afternoon safaris explore the reserve's zones, among them Tala, Magadhi and Khitauli. In Tala, the track climbs beneath the sandstone cliffs of the ancient fort to Shesh Shaiya, a reclining stone Vishnu watched over by langurs, past ancient rock-cut caves.

    Overnight: Mahua Kothi (Taj Safaris), Bandhavgarh

  10. Day 10: Bandhavgarh National Park

    Another day in the forest, with the chance of tigresses with cubs, leopards and sloth bears. Your naturalist shares the stories of the tigers who rule here today, many descended from legends such as Charger and Sita. In the afternoon, you might choose a quieter drive in the buffer zone, where leopards and birdlife are often seen.

    Overnight: Mahua Kothi (Taj Safaris), Bandhavgarh

  11. Day 11: Bandhavgarh National Park

    A final full day of safaris gives you your best chance of the sightings Bandhavgarh is famous for. In the evening, a private farewell dinner is served beneath the mahua trees, with your naturalist sharing stories from a lifetime in the forest.

    Overnight: Mahua Kothi (Taj Safaris), Bandhavgarh

  12. Day 12: Bandhavgarh to New Delhi

    After a leisurely breakfast, your chauffeur drives you to Jabalpur, about four hours away, for your flight to Delhi. If flight times allow, stop at Bhedaghat, where the Narmada River runs between gleaming marble cliffs. Your final evening is at The Oberoi New Delhi.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  13. Day 13: Departure

    Your chauffeur drives you to Indira Gandhi International Airport for your onward journey.

A note from our planners

The Madhya Pradesh reserves rest on Wednesday afternoons and close for Holi and Diwali. Your itinerary is arranged around these dates, and an in-lodge experience takes the place of any affected drive.

National Parks & Wildlife Reserves

  • Pench National Park: Named for the river that flows through it, Pench spreads across the Seoni hills in a mosaic of teak forest, bamboo and open grassland. Its abundant deer support healthy numbers of tigers, leopards and dhole, and its landscapes inspired Kipling's tales of Mowgli.
  • Kanha National Park: One of Asia's great protected landscapes, Kanha pairs towering sal forest with sweeping meadows in the Maikal hills. It is celebrated for saving the hard-ground barasingha from extinction and for excellent sightings of tigers, dhole and gaur.
  • Bandhavgarh National Park: A compact reserve of rugged hills, sal forest and grassy valleys, Bandhavgarh is overlooked by an ancient fort linked in legend to the Ramayana. It has one of the highest tiger densities in India.

Pench, Kanha & Bandhavgarh Tiger Safari FAQs

What is the best order to visit Pench, Kanha and Bandhavgarh?

Most travellers begin in Pench, flying into Nagpur, and finish in Bandhavgarh, flying home from Jabalpur. This route avoids any backtracking and saves the longest stays for Kanha and Bandhavgarh.

How many days do I need for a tiger safari in Pench, Kanha and Bandhavgarh?

We recommend around twelve nights, as on this tour: two in Pench, four each in Kanha and Bandhavgarh, and a night in Delhi at each end. Our safari specialists can shorten the route if your time is limited.

When is the best time for a Central India tiger safari?

The parks are open from October to June. November to March brings cool, comfortable days, while the warmer months from April to June are often the most rewarding for tiger sightings, as animals gather near water.

Are the parks open every day?

The Madhya Pradesh reserves close to safaris on Wednesday afternoons and for the festivals of Holi and Diwali. We plan your safaris around these dates.

What are my chances of seeing a tiger?

Tigers are wild, so sightings can never be guaranteed. With four nights each in Kanha and Bandhavgarh, two of India's finest tiger reserves, and expert naturalists at your side, your chances are excellent.
