About This Tiger & Lion Safari

This tiger and lion safari in India brings together two of the world's great cats on one western India journey. In Ranthambore, tigers move through dry forest and past the lakes and pavilions of a thousand-year-old fort. In Gir, on the Saurashtra peninsula of Gujarat, lives the last wild population of the Asiatic lion anywhere on earth, a species that numbered only a handful at the end of the 19th century and has grown to 891 lions in the 2025 estimate.

You stay at two of western India's finest safari retreats. Aman-i-Khas, a camp of ten tents inspired by the Mughal encampments of old, lies on the edge of Ranthambore. In Gir, The Postcard Gir is an intimate retreat of just 15 rooms, set in 16 acres of mango orchard on the edge of the forest. Every safari is taken in a private 4x4 jeep, reserved for your party alone, with naturalists who know each forest intimately. A VIP welcome at Delhi airport, chauffeur-driven private SUVs and domestic flights make every transfer effortless.

This is travel that gives back. Our partner lodges employ and train people from neighbouring villages, our naturalists follow the forest department's code of conduct at every sighting, and your park fees help protect the reserves themselves. In Gir, the lions' recovery owes much to the Maldhari herders who have shared the forest with them for generations. Your journey also contributes to TOFTigers, the wildlife tourism and conservation alliance that won a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025. The best time to travel is November to April, and every departure is private and bespoke, curated around your dates and interests.

What You Will Experience

  • Tigers and Asiatic lions in one journey, from the forests of Rajasthan to the last wild home of the Asiatic lion.
  • Private 4x4 jeep safaris at dawn and dusk, reserved for your party alone and led by expert naturalists.

Ranthambore & Gir Itinerary: Day by Day

  1. Day 1: Arrival in New Delhi

    A VIP representative meets you at the aircraft and guides you swiftly through immigration, baggage reclaim and customs. Your chauffeur drives you to The Oberoi New Delhi, where the rest of the day is yours to rest after your flight.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  2. Day 2: New Delhi to Ranthambore National Park

    A short morning flight takes you to Jaipur, where your chauffeur is waiting for the drive of about three hours through the Rajasthan countryside to Ranthambore. Aman-i-Khas welcomes you with lunch in its dining tent, then the afternoon is yours to settle into your tent, each with its own sitting room and bathing area. In the evening, gather around the fire beneath the stars.

    Overnight: Aman-i-Khas, Ranthambore

  3. Day 3: Ranthambore National Park

    Morning and afternoon safaris take you through dry forest, rocky ridges and grassland to the lakes of Padam Talao, Rajbagh and Malik Talao, where tigers come to drink and sambar wade among the water plants. Your naturalist follows alarm calls and fresh pugmarks, while mugger crocodiles bask on the banks and leopards rest on the rocky slopes above.

    Overnight: Aman-i-Khas, Ranthambore

  4. Day 4: Ranthambore National Park

    After a dawn safari, spend the late morning at leisure in camp or at the spa tent. In the afternoon, choose between a final safari or a visit to Ranthambore Fort, part of the UNESCO-listed Hill Forts of Rajasthan, where the walls and temples look out across the forest.

    Overnight: Aman-i-Khas, Ranthambore

  5. Day 5: Ranthambore to New Delhi

    After breakfast, drive back to Jaipur for your flight to Delhi. Tonight you stay at Roseate House in Aerocity, minutes from the airport, ready for tomorrow's flight west.

    Overnight: Roseate House, New Delhi

  6. Day 6: New Delhi to Gir National Park

    A morning flight carries you to Rajkot, in the Saurashtra peninsula of Gujarat, and your chauffeur drives you south for about four hours through farmland and mango orchards to Gir. The Postcard Gir lies on the edge of the forest, its 15 spacious rooms set among 16 acres of mango trees, with soft, light interiors and views across the orchard. Spend the evening by the pool or on your veranda as peafowl call from the trees.

    Overnight: The Postcard Gir

  7. Day 7: Gir National Park

    Set out at dawn on the Gir Jungle Trail, following forest roads through teak, scrub and grassland in search of lions. Prides often rest in the open or beside the track, and your naturalist explains how the population has recovered from just a handful of animals. Leopards, striped hyenas, chital, nilgai and chinkara also share these forests. In the afternoon, a second safari explores another route.

    Overnight: The Postcard Gir

  8. Day 8: Gir National Park

    A morning safari is followed by a visit to a Maldhari settlement, where herders who have lived alongside the lions for generations share their way of life. Later, watch mugger crocodiles basking at Kamleshwar Dam on the Hiran River, deep inside the sanctuary. Dinner tonight is served in the lodge's mango orchard.

    Overnight: The Postcard Gir

  9. Day 9: Gir National Park and Somnath

    Enjoy a final morning safari in Gir. In the afternoon, drive about an hour to the coast at Somnath, where the great temple stands above the Arabian Sea, and stay for the evening aarti as lamps are lit at dusk.

    Overnight: The Postcard Gir

  10. Day 10: Gir to New Delhi

    After a leisurely breakfast, drive back to Rajkot for your flight to Delhi. Your final evening is at The Oberoi New Delhi.

    Overnight: The Oberoi New Delhi

  11. Day 11: Departure

    Your chauffeur drives you to Indira Gandhi International Airport for your onward journey.

A note from our planners

Gir National Park is open from 16 October to 15 June, and Aman-i-Khas from October to May, so this journey runs between those months. Safaris in both parks follow routes and permits set by the forest department, and we secure them well in advance.

National Parks & Wildlife Reserves

  • Ranthambore National Park: Dry forest, rocky ridges and lakes in eastern Rajasthan, beneath a 10th-century fort that forms part of the UNESCO-listed Hill Forts of Rajasthan. It is home to some of India's most photographed tigers.
  • Gir National Park and Wildlife Sanctuary: About 1,410 sq km of teak forest, scrub and grassland in Gujarat, the only place in the world where wild Asiatic lions live. Once the hunting ground of the Nawab of Junagadh, it became a sanctuary in 1965, and the 2025 estimate recorded 891 lions across the wider Saurashtra landscape.

Tiger & Asiatic Lion Safari FAQs

When is the best time for a tiger and lion safari in India?

November to April. Gir is open from 16 October to 15 June and Aman-i-Khas from October to May. November to March brings cool, comfortable days, while April is warmer and often excellent for sightings, as animals gather near water.

What are my chances of seeing lions and tigers?

Lion sightings in Gir are frequent, as prides often rest in the open beside the track, and Ranthambore is one of the best places in India to see a tiger. Wild animals can never be guaranteed, but with three nights in Ranthambore and four in Gir, your chances are excellent.

How do we travel between Ranthambore and Gir?

The two parks lie in different states, so the simplest route is through Delhi. You fly from Jaipur to Delhi, spend a night near the airport, then take a morning flight to Rajkot and drive about four hours to Gir.

Is this tiger and lion safari suitable for families?

Yes. Both lodges welcome families, and the shorter guided drives at Devalia in Gir suit children who are new to safaris. Our safari specialists can adjust the pace for your group.

How do I book a private tiger and lion safari?

Speak with our safari specialists, who will tailor this itinerary around your dates, interests and pace. Every journey is private, bespoke and curated for you, and we recommend booking six to nine months ahead for the best choice of lodges and park permits.
