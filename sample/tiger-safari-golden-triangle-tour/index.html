About This Tiger Safari

This Tiger Safari & Golden Triangle Tour pairs India's three great monuments with three days in Ranthambore National Park, where tigers hunt among the ruins of a tenth-century fort. In ten days you move from the Mughal grandeur of Delhi and the Taj Mahal at dawn to Jaipur's palaces and the forests of Rajasthan, in comfortable, chauffeur-driven private SUVs, with a VIP meet and greet on arrival.

Every journey is private and curated for you alone. Choose one of three collections, The Experiential Collection, The Heritage Collection or The Palace & Camp Collection, and your bespoke programme stays the same while the hotels and lodges change to suit the way you like to travel.

Each journey contributes to TOFTigers, winner of a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025, supporting responsible tiger tourism and the communities that live beside the forest.

What You Will Experience

  • Two private game drives a day in Ranthambore with an expert naturalist, the best chance of a daylight tiger sighting
  • The Taj Mahal at sunset on arrival and again at sunrise, before the crowds
  • Amber Fort, the City Palace and Hawa Mahal with a private guide
  • Old Delhi's lanes and Humayun's Tomb
  • Fatehpur Sikri and the Chand Baori stepwell at Abhaneri
  • Ranthambore Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site shared with langurs and peacocks

Tiger Safari & Golden Triangle Itinerary: Day by Day

Day 1: Arrive in Delhi

Stay: Colonel's Retreat ITC Maurya The Oberoi, New Delhi Delhi
Photograph to come

A VIP meet and greet at the airport, then a chauffeur-driven private SUV to your hotel. The rest of the day is yours; our concierge can arrange an early dinner.

Day 2: Old Delhi and Humayun's Tomb

Stay: Colonel's Retreat ITC Maurya The Oberoi, New Delhi Delhi
Photograph to come

A private guide leads you through Old Delhi's lanes around Jama Masjid, then Lutyens' Delhi and Humayun's Tomb, the garden tomb that inspired the Taj Mahal, with a long break in the afternoon.

Day 3: Delhi to Agra and Agra Fort

Stay: Tajview ITC Mughal The Oberoi Amarvilas Agra Drive: about 4 hours (230 km)
Photograph to come

Cross the Yamuna Expressway to Agra and visit Agra Fort, then watch the Taj Mahal glow at sunset from Mehtab Bagh, across the river.

Day 4: Taj Mahal at Sunrise and Drive to Jaipur

Stay: Dera Jaipur Jai Mahal Palace Rambagh Palace Jaipur Drive: about 5 hours (240 km)
Photograph to come

See the Taj Mahal when it opens, in soft light and thin crowds. Then drive to Jaipur via Fatehpur Sikri, Akbar's abandoned capital, and the Chand Baori stepwell at Abhaneri.

Day 5: Amber Fort and the Pink City of Jaipur

Stay: Dera Jaipur Jai Mahal Palace Rambagh Palace Jaipur
Photograph to come

Amber Fort before the heat, then the City Palace, Jantar Mantar and the Hawa Mahal. The late afternoon is at leisure, or with block-printing and jewellery artisans.

Day 6: Jaipur to Ranthambore National Park

Stay: Dev Vilas Khem Villas Sujan Sher Bagh Ranthambore Drive: about 4 hours (180 km)
Photograph to come

Arrive at Ranthambore National Park in time for lunch, then your naturalist briefs you on the park, its zones and the tigers that roam them.

Day 7: Tiger Safari in Ranthambore

Stay: Dev Vilas Khem Villas Sujan Sher Bagh Ranthambore
Photograph to come

Two tiger safari game drives, one at first light and one in the afternoon, with an expert naturalist. Ranthambore is one of the few reserves where tigers are regularly seen in daylight around lakes, ruins and open grassland.

Day 8: Ranthambore Game Drives and Ranthambore Fort

Stay: Dev Vilas Khem Villas Sujan Sher Bagh Ranthambore
Photograph to come

A second day of game drives in different zones, with a guided visit to Ranthambore Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, between them.

Day 9: Ranthambore to Delhi

Stay: Colonel's Retreat ITC Maurya The Oberoi, New Delhi Delhi Drive: about 7 hours, or by train from Sawai Madhopur, which we can arrange
Photograph to come

A final early game drive, then the journey back to Delhi. Our concierge can book a farewell dinner in the capital.

Day 10: Departure from Delhi
Photograph to come

A chauffeur-driven private SUV takes you to the airport, with a VIP escort through departures.

Popular Fauna
  • Mammals: Bengal tiger, leopard, sloth bear, striped hyena, sambar, chital, nilgai, wild boar, jungle cat, Indian fox, rhesus macaque and common langur
  • Reptiles: marsh crocodile and monitor lizard
  • Birds: crested serpent eagle, Indian eagle-owl, painted spurfowl, Indian peafowl, spotted owlet and kingfishers around the lakes

Sightings vary by season and are never guaranteed, but tigers are seen regularly in daylight.

National Parks & Wildlife Reserves

Ranthambore National Park (Rajasthan): about 392 sq km of dry deciduous forest, lakes and ruins, centred on a tenth-century fort. The park is open from October to June and closed during the monsoon. See our Ranthambore National Park guide.

UNESCO World Heritage Sites
  • Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, Agra
  • Humayun's Tomb, Delhi
  • Fatehpur Sikri, near Agra
  • Amber Fort and Jantar Mantar, Jaipur
  • Ranthambore Fort, part of the Hill Forts of Rajasthan

Tiger Safari & Golden Triangle Tour FAQs

When is the best time for a tiger safari and Golden Triangle tour?

October to March is the most comfortable season for the monuments, and Ranthambore is open from October to June. Tigers are often easier to see in the warmer months of April to June, when animals visit the waterholes.

How likely am I to see a tiger in Ranthambore?

Ranthambore is among the most reliable parks in India for tiger sightings, and daylight sightings are common. With two game drives on each of three days, you have six chances, though sightings are never guaranteed.

What should I know about the practicalities?

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays, so we plan the Agra day around it. Drives are long but comfortable: Delhi to Agra is about 4 hours, Agra to Jaipur about 5 hours and Jaipur to Ranthambore about 4 hours. Carry photo identification for park entry.

Is this tour suitable for families and first-time visitors to India?

Yes. The pace is gentle, the vehicles are private and our safari specialists can adapt the programme for children, older travellers or serious photographers.

How do I book a bespoke tiger safari and Golden Triangle tour?

Contact our safari specialists, who will curate the journey around your dates, your collection of choice and any special occasions. All tours are private.
