Tiger Conservation in India: Our Work with TOFTigers

Every journey we design is private, bespoke and curated around the wellbeing of the forests it visits.

A Founder on the Board of TOFTigers

Our founder serves as a director of TOFTigers, the non-profit that sets the standard for responsible tiger tourism in South Asia. We know what TOFTigers expects of lodges and operators from the inside, and we hold ourselves to it.

What Is TOFTigers?

TOFTigers (Travel Operators for Tigers) was founded by Julian Matthews and Vishal Singh, and the campaign launched in 2004 to encourage more sustainable nature tourism in tiger parks. Its premise is simple: responsible, well-managed tourism gives local people an economic reason to protect tigers and their forests.

  • A global network. More than 220 travel businesses worldwide are involved.
  • A rigorous eco-rating. Its PUG (Practices Under Guidelines) scoring system holds participating lodges, hotels and resorts to account.
  • Training for local guides. Its programmes strengthen the knowledge and skills of guides in the wilderness.
  • Recognition of excellence. The TOFTigers Wildlife Awards, established in 2010, celebrate tourism that benefits wildlife and communities.

TOFTigers received the Wind Under the Wings Award from Sanctuary Nature Foundation in 2007 and a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025. Its founder points to Ranthambore, where tigers have risen from 11 in 2004 to more than 60, as proof of what well-managed tourism can support.
