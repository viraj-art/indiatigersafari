A note from the founder

Why Travel with India Tiger Safari?

  1. 01

    Curated Experiences

    Each itinerary is composed around you, whether you dream of a first tiger sighting, a family adventure or a dedicated wildlife photography expedition. Every detail is considered, nothing is left to chance.

  2. 02

    Specialist Knowledge

    We know which reserves shine in which season, and how to link them gracefully, so that your hours are spent in the forest rather than on the road.

  3. 03

    Handpicked, Effortlessly Luxurious Stays

    From the intimate safari lodges of Taj Safaris, Pugdundee Safaris and Aman to the Oberoi hotels and palace stays of the cities, each address is one we have trusted over many years.

  4. 04

    Seamless from Arrival to Departure

    A VIP meet and greet, comfortable chauffeur-driven private SUVs, domestic flights and full board at the safari lodges: every practical detail is taken care of, quietly and in advance.

  5. 05

    Travel That Gives Back

    Each journey contributes to TOFTigers, winner of a Travel + Leisure Global Vision Award in 2025, supporting the conservation of tigers and the communities who live beside the forests.

National parks

All national park guides

  • Uttarakhand

    Jim Corbett

    India's first national park, in the Himalayan foothills.

  • Rajasthan

    Ranthambore

    Tigers among lakes, banyans and the ruins of a royal past.

  • Madhya Pradesh

    Kanha

    Towering sal forest and wide grassy meadows in the Maikal hills.

  • Madhya Pradesh

    Bandhavgarh

    Sandstone cliffs, an ancient fort and one of the highest tiger densities in India.

  • Madhya Pradesh

    Pench

    Teak forests in the Seoni hills, the setting of Kipling's The Jungle Book.

  • Ladakh

    Hemis

    A high world of scree slopes and frozen streams, home of the snow leopard.
